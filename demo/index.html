7Limits and Improvements

These are the limits of this project and ways to improve it.

Where It Gets It Wrong

  • Every short dip is reported as multipath. There were 305 on the evaluation day, and most were at the row ends with all three tractors dipping together.
  • Without per-satellite data we can't tell row-end shading from true multipath.
  • The cutoffs come from each tractor's own normal and from calm development minutes, never from evaluation.
  • Using development data we can fit our solution to work perfectly on that data, but real life data is messy.
  • Not every combination of two or three causes has been handled or tested. The labels only show a few of them.
  • Jammer on one tractor → equipment fault
  • Base-station outage → three equipment faults
  • Signal fault during a storm → space weather
  • This runs on recorded data after the fact. In a real scenario we would want it to run in real time.

Ways to Improve

  • AGC and noise floor: tells jamming from an antenna fault
  • Per-satellite signal with satellite IDs: tells row-end shading from multipath
  • More labeled examples of multipath and of overlapping causes
